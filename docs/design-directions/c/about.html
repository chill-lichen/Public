About

Hi, I’m Ravi.

I started in regulatory compliance and financial risk, moved into data and technology, and stayed because the problems kept changing.

Much of my work has been getting people with different priorities to agree: business teams, engineers, risk and executives. I’m not always the deepest specialist in the room. I’m comfortable finding out what I need to know.
