# Unified website design preview

**Preview URL (after Pages deployment):** https://chill-lichen.github.io/Public/unified/

**Live domain is unchanged:** https://www.raviyeleswarapu.org/

The staging website consolidates the personal-professional identity and owner-focused reporting services under one visual and editorial system. It is intentionally hosted in its own folder; none of the current root site, apex DNS, Webflow setup or other published pages were replaced.

## Pages
- Home — specific owner problems, example data visualization, and prior employment evidence
- Services — three scopes: understanding job/service costs, improving reporting, and documenting handoffs
- Sample Work — working, six-month fictional owner report with selectable months, reconciled figures and downloadable CSV
- About — personal background, qualifications, previous-employer evidence, and LinkedIn
- Contact — project-intake form on Notion plus email
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Service — current legal copy carried into the shared design; SMS remains a future service

## Design system
- One wordmark: Ravi Yeleswarapu
- Descriptive tagline, not a separate business name: Reporting & Business Systems
- Ink (#182338), warm cream (#F6F3EC), blue (#3E62BC), restrained clay (#BD7957)
- Editorial serif headings with familiar sans-serif body copy
- Purposeful illustrations and functional example data, not stock imagery or fabricated customer stories
- Reusable navigation, footer, spacing, buttons and page sections, including mobile layout

## Source integrity
- Owner-report figures are entirely fictional and clearly labeled. The CSV reconciles from a beginning unpaid-invoice balance of $8,600: closing unpaid = opening unpaid + invoiced - collected.
- Direct service-delivery costs are not total expenses or net profit. June service contributions are illustrative and do not include overhead.
- NXT Capital and BMO statistics come from the user's LinkedIn résumé export and are attributed to prior employment, not clients of this practice.
- The site doesn't advertise active M&A brokerage, formal valuation, accounting, tax, securities or regulated financial-planning services.
- Staging has no visitor analytics script and is flagged noindex/nofollow. No confidential files or payment information are requested.
