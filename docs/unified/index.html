Why me?

I started my career in regulatory compliance. I'm comfortable working inside the rules.

My degree is in financial counseling and planning, and my first years were spent consulting on banking regulation and risk at Capgemini and Capco. After that I spent most of a decade in data and reporting. At NXT Capital I helped cut a monthly reporting process from five days to one. At BMO I worked on executive reporting automation that reduced manual effort by about 90%. Those were results in previous jobs, not client results for this practice.

Paraplanning as an outside service is new for me, and I'd rather say that plainly. I'd suggest starting with a small, well-defined piece of work so you can judge the quality before giving me more.
