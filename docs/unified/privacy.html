Website policies

Privacy Policy

What information is collected through this website and what it's used for.

Effective: November 8, 2026 · Contact: info@raviyeleswarapu.org

Working policy text for the design preview. Review against the final hosting, analytics and messaging setup before launch.

At a glance: This is a small professional-services website operated by Ravi Yeleswarapu, under the descriptive name “Reporting & Business Systems.” You may browse without submitting an inquiry. We use information you choose to provide to assess project requests and reply. This design preview does not run website visitor analytics or advertising trackers.

1. Who operates this website

“We,” “us,” and “our” refer to Ravi Yeleswarapu operating this professional-services website. “Reporting & Business Systems” is a descriptive site name and does not, by itself, represent a separately registered corporation or LLC. This policy covers the website and project inquiries made through it.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: Your name, email address, optional organization, the type of help requested, project description, desired outcome, tools involved, preferred deadline, optional budget, and permission to contact you, as entered on the Notion project intake form.
  • Communications: Information you send through email, including your contact details, inquiry, and subsequent replies.
  • Website and analytics data: Our hosting and service providers may process ordinary technical information necessary to deliver pages. This preview does not run Apollo analytics. The website hosting provider may process technical data such as request logs to serve the site. If optional analytics is introduced on the live website, this notice and the consent mechanism will be updated first.
  • Future text messaging, only if introduced: A mobile number, SMS-specific consent record, and message history may be processed if you separately choose to enroll in a text-message program. The current project intake form does not enroll visitors in SMS.

3. How information is used

We use submitted information to review inquiries, decide whether a project is a fit, communicate about possible services, develop proposals, provide services if separately agreed, maintain records relevant to those activities, protect against abuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not treat a project intake submission as consent to receive marketing texts. The current preview does not ask for analytics consent or load the Apollo tracker.

4. Services that process information

  • Website hosting: GitHub Pages serves the current design preview; Netlify may serve the same website from its connected GitHub repository. The provider serving the page can process ordinary technical information necessary to deliver it. The final policy will identify the production host once the custom domain moves.
  • Notion: Hosts the separately opened project intake form and stores submitted inquiries in a private Notion project database. Visiting the form may result in Notion collecting technical information under its own privacy practices.
  • Email providers: Process messages when you choose to contact us by email or when we reply.
  • Optional future analytics: The current unified preview does not load Apollo or other visitor tracking. If analytics is enabled later, we will describe the provider and its data processing before activation.
  • Future messaging providers: If an SMS program is introduced, a business communications provider may process numbers and messages as necessary to deliver and support the communications you requested.

We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or prevent fraud. We do not sell personal information or use information submitted in a project inquiry to create a lead list for sale.

5. Mobile information and SMS consent

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers, messaging opt-in records, and text-message consent will not be shared with third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Service providers may process information only as needed to support messages you have chosen to receive.

There is currently no SMS enrollment on the public project intake form. If text messaging becomes available, we will describe the types of messages, request separate optional consent, explain how to stop messages, and maintain appropriate consent records. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. For messaging terms, see the SMS Terms.

6. Cookies, local storage, and analytics choices

This design preview does not load Apollo analytics, use advertising pixels, or store an analytics consent choice. If optional analytics is enabled on a future live version of the site, the associated script will require a suitable consent mechanism. Notion and other external sites that you choose to open may use their own technologies and privacy policies.

7. Retention and security

Inquiry and communication records are kept only as reasonably necessary to evaluate requests, conduct any agreed work, maintain appropriate business records, resolve issues, or comply with legal obligations. You may ask us to review or delete an inquiry, subject to requirements that may permit or require retention. We use access controls available from our service providers, but no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, confidential client documents, regulated health information, or sensitive financial account information through the initial intake.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide optional information and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted. Applicable privacy laws may provide additional rights. Send requests to info@raviyeleswarapu.org. We may need to verify that a request concerns your information before acting.

9. Children and external websites

This website is intended for adults seeking professional services and is not directed to children. Links to third-party sites, including Notion, are governed by their respective privacy practices when you visit them.

10. Updates and contact

We may revise this policy as site features, vendors, or legal requirements change. The effective date above indicates the current version. For privacy questions, contact info@raviyeleswarapu.org.
