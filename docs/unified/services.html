Services

A report you can trust. A process you can repeat.

The starting point is usually something you already do: monthly reporting, a spreadsheet that's hard to maintain, or a workflow that keeps pulling the owner back in. Here are the kinds of projects I can take on.

01

Find out what the work actually costs

You know which clients bring in the most revenue. But what happens when you count the hours, subcontractors, and revisions? I can help pull together the records and build a useful comparison—not a full accounting system.

What we'd work from

Invoices, project lists, time records, direct costs, and the way you classify your services.

What you could take away

An owner report comparing jobs or services, an explanation of the calculations, and a way to update it monthly.

02

Fix a report that takes too long

If making the monthly report involves exporting five files, pasting figures into Excel, and correcting broken formulas, we can start there. Sometimes the right fix is a cleaner spreadsheet, not another platform.

What we'd work from

The current spreadsheet, the source exports, how often you need the report, and who relies on it.

What you could take away

A working report or workbook, checks for obvious errors, and instructions another person can follow.

03

Get a recurring process out of your head

Work stops because one person knows the next step. Let's document how requests arrive, who does what, and what happens when something goes wrong.

What we'd work from

Your current tools, a recent real example, and the people who handle each step.

What you could take away

A process map, handoff checklist, responsibilities, and a short list of fixes worth trying first.

What this isn't: bookkeeping, tax preparation, audit services, investment advice, formal valuations, or selling your business. I can work alongside your accountant or other advisers where appropriate, but I don't replace them.
