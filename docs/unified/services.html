Services

Paraplanning and bookkeeping, done for the advisor.

I support independent financial advisors and small RIAs with the preparation and record-keeping around client work. I work under your direction and in your systems where possible. You review what I prepare, and you make every recommendation.

01

Paraplanning support

The groundwork before and after a planning meeting: collecting and organizing client information, entering it into your planning software, running the scenarios you specify, and assembling materials for you to review. I can also help with the follow-up after the meeting.

What we'd work from

Client statements and data you provide, your planning software and templates, your notes on the client's goals, and the scenarios you want modeled.

What you could take away

An organized client file, data entered and checked, draft plan outputs and meeting packets for your review, and meeting notes and task lists in your CRM.

02

Preparation for business-owner clients

I hold the Certified Exit Planning Advisor (CEPA) credential, and I've spent time around small-business finances. For your clients who own businesses, I can help organize the information an exit or succession conversation depends on, so your meetings start further along.

What we'd work from

The business's financial statements and records, your client's answers to readiness questions, and the outline you want for the conversation.

What you could take away

Organized business financials, a summary of open questions and gaps, and draft materials for you and the client's other professionals to review.

03

Bookkeeping for your firm

Your practice's own books, kept current. I can categorize transactions, reconcile bank and card accounts, tie advisory fee revenue back to your billing or custodian reports, and close each month so your numbers are ready when your CPA needs them.

What we'd work from

Your accounting software, bank and card feeds, billing or custodian fee reports, and receipts.

What you could take away

Reconciled accounts, a monthly close with a short summary of anything unusual, and organized records for your CPA.

How the work is supervised: I work as a contractor for your firm, and you remain responsible for all advice and client communication. I don't make recommendations to your clients, and nothing I prepare goes to a client without your review. I'll follow your firm's data-security and confidentiality policies and sign your confidentiality agreement. I am not a registered investment adviser or investment adviser representative. I don't prepare tax returns, perform audits, give legal advice, produce formal business valuations, or broker business sales.
