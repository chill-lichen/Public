About

Hi, I'm Ravi.

I've had a fairly unplanned career. I started in regulatory compliance and financial risk, wandered into data and technology, and stayed because the problems kept changing.

Much of my work has involved bringing people with different priorities to some kind of agreement—business teams, architects, engineers, risk, and executives. I'm not always the deepest specialist in the room. I'm comfortable finding out what I need to know.

Professional history

The work, in order.

Different titles, overlapping interests. The short version is below; the fuller employment history is on LinkedIn.

  1. 2013–2017

    Capgemini & Capco

    Consulting in banking regulation, financial risk, and compliance. My first introduction to how complicated organizations actually work.

  2. 2017–2021

    BMO

    Analytics capabilities and reporting. Worked with teams automating executive reports and reducing the manual effort behind them.

  3. 2021–2022

    Epsilon

    Product management for measurement and reporting. Different industry, familiar arguments about what the numbers mean.

  4. 2022–2024

    BMO, again

    Product ownership across data and technology, including an MLOps platform. A lot of work aligning architects, engineers, and business stakeholders.

  5. 2024–2025

    NXT Capital

    Data and reporting. Helped reduce a monthly reporting process from five days to one, alongside governance and controls work.

  6. 2026

    A detour into business ownership

    I started a travel agency, an insurance agency, and a business brokerage at the same time. I thought the three made sense together. In practice, none found its footing, and I closed them.

  7. Now

    Small projects, and what's next

    I'm taking on reporting and workflow projects for owner-run businesses, and giving more thought to what I want to build next.

Examples above are from previous employment, not work completed for this practice. My CEPA and CM&AP credentials relate to my background; this website does not offer regulated advice, formal business valuations, or brokerage engagements.
