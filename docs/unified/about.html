About

I'm Ravi. I work on the part between the data and the decision.

My background is in financial services, reporting and data systems. I've also worked in business brokerage. I'm using those experiences to offer narrower, hands-on projects for people running smaller businesses.

How I got here

I know how much work hides behind a monthly report.

At BMO, NXT Capital and Epsilon, I worked with teams that needed reliable numbers—and with the technology and processes required to produce them. The hard part wasn't usually drawing the chart. It was agreeing on what the numbers meant, getting the right data, and making sure the report could be produced again.

At NXT Capital, my work included standardizing reporting and playbooks and helping reduce a monthly reporting cycle from five days to one. At BMO, I worked on automated executive reporting that reduced manual effort by about 90%. Those are earlier employer results, not promises for a new client.

In business brokerage, I saw another side of the same problem: owners trying to explain how their business makes money, what it depends on, and how reliable the records are. I earned CEPA and CM&AP credentials and hold a B.S. in Financial Counseling and Planning from Purdue University.

This services practice focuses on business reporting and operating processes. It does not offer investment advice, tax preparation, formal valuations, or brokerage work.

How I work

You'll work with the person doing the job.

I'm not selling a large team or a new software subscription. I would rather start with one hard-to-answer question, understand the data and process behind it, and work out whether a small project is enough to fix it.

If the work requires a bookkeeper, CPA, attorney, licensed adviser or larger implementation team, I'll tell you.
