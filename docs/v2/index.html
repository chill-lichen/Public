Questions I can help you answer

Your accounting software records what happened. It doesn't always explain why.

The answer may be split between invoices, timesheets, project records and spreadsheets. Getting them together is the hard part.

01 / COST OF THE WORK

“Which clients actually make us money?”

Put revenue next to hours and delivery costs. The biggest client isn't always the most profitable.

02 / CASH

“We're busy. Why is cash still tight?”

See what's been invoiced, what's still unpaid, and what obligations are coming due before deciding what you can spend.

03 / THE DAILY HANDOFFS

“Why does everyone still have to ask me?”

Get the steps and responsibilities out of email threads and your head, so people can keep moving when you're unavailable.

What I do

Start with one report or one broken process.

Some businesses need a better view of their numbers. Others just need to stop copying the same data by hand every week. These are examples of the work I can take on.

01 / BUSINESS REPORTING

See where the money goes

Compare revenue, cash collections, and time spent across projects or services. Find out where the picture is incomplete or misleading.

What you could get: A monthly owner report, with numbers you can trace back to the source.

02 / SPREADSHEETS & DATA

Fix the reporting routine

Clean up spreadsheets, reconcile mismatched figures, and reduce the manual steps involved in producing the same report again.

What you could get: A working workbook or reporting workflow, plus instructions for updating it.

03 / HOW WORK GETS DONE

Make it easier to delegate

Map how work arrives, moves between people, and gets finished. Then document the important steps and simplify where possible.

What you could get: A process map, checklist, and a clear owner for each handoff.

Why hire me?

I've done more than put charts on a screen.

I'm Ravi Yeleswarapu. For more than a decade I've worked with data, reporting, and the people who rely on it—at BMO, Epsilon and NXT Capital. That includes figuring out why numbers don't agree, building reports people can trust, and making the next reporting cycle less painful.

At NXT Capital, I helped reduce a monthly reporting process from five days to one. At BMO, I worked on executive reporting automation that reduced the effort involved by about 90%. Those were projects in larger organizations, not promises about what I can do for yours.

I also spent time in business brokerage and earned the CEPA and CM&AP designations. That experience taught me how much it matters to understand the numbers behind a business—and to be able to explain how it runs. This website is for reporting and operating systems, not investment advice, valuations, or brokerage engagements.
