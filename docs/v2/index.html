Sound familiar?

Three questions that get harder as the business gets busier.

When you're doing the work and running the company, important answers can end up buried in invoices, reports, and your own memory.

01 / MARGIN

“Which clients or services actually make us money?”

Revenue tells one story. Time spent, delivery costs, and rework tell another. Compare them before deciding what to sell more of.

02 / CASH

“Can I afford another hire—or should I wait?”

Look at cash coming in, payments going out, and commitments on the horizon, so the decision isn't based on last month's sales alone.

03 / DEPENDENCY

“What breaks if I take two weeks off?”

If the process lives in your head, it's hard to delegate. Document the handoffs, owners, and next steps so work doesn't stall.

Where we could begin

A focused first project beats an open-ended consulting engagement.

We can start with one decision you need to make or one process that's costing too much effort. The scope is defined before work begins.

01 / Business visibility

Know your numbers

Find out which services contribute to revenue, where cash gets held up, and which operating measures deserve attention.

Possible output: A management scorecard with a documented monthly refresh.

02 / Reporting routines

Put reporting on repeat

Stop rebuilding the same spreadsheet or report every month. Make it easier to collect, check, and present the information you use.

Possible output: A cleaner workbook or recurring reporting workflow with handoff notes.

03 / Owner dependency

Make the work easier to hand off

Identify the steps only you know, document the critical handoffs, and make it easier for another person to keep the work moving.

Possible output: A workflow map, operating checklist, and prioritized improvements.
